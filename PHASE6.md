# SkillLink Phase 6 — Instructor Dashboard

Implemented:
- Instructor-only server-side authorization.
- Instructor dashboard.
- Course draft creation.
- Course draft editing.
- Lesson creation with ownership checks.
- Course submission for approval.
- Minimum one-lesson submission validation.
- Instructor masterclass draft creation.
- Instructor workshop draft creation.
- Own-course student count.
- Verified paid course-order count/value visibility.
- Audit logging for course creation/submission.
- Responsive Instructor Studio UI.
- No instructor ability to directly publish courses.
- No fake student counts, sales or earnings are generated.

Important:
- Approval remains a privileged platform operation.
- Instructor cannot modify another instructor's course.
- Published courses are protected from direct instructor editing through this API.
- Payment and commerce remain in Phase 9.
- Detailed student communication/Q&A is not implemented yet.
